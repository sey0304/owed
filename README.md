# Owed

Your flight was late. The airline may owe you up to €600.

Type where you flew, how late you were, and what the airline said. Owed works out whether EU rules cover you, how much you are owed, and writes the claim letter for you.

## Why

Claim companies do this for you and keep about a third of the money. On a €600 claim that is over €200 gone. Owed writes the same letter and you keep all of it.

## Features

- 7,900+ airports, searchable by 3-letter code or city name
- Works out the flight distance and the amount (€250 / €400 / €600)
- Knows which excuses an airline can and cannot use
- Writes a claim letter in English, ready to copy and send
- Nothing you type ever leaves your browser

## The rules it uses

Regulation (EC) 261/2004. You are owed compensation when you arrive at your final destination three hours or more late, and the delay was not caused by something outside the airline's control.

| Flight distance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,500 km | €250 |
| 1,500–3,500 km | €400 |
| Over 3,500 km | €600 |

Bad weather, air traffic control strikes and security threats are outside the airline's control. A technical fault, a crew problem or overbooking is not.

## Read this first

This is a guide, not legal advice. It was not written by a lawyer. Check the official page before you claim: [Your Europe – Air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm)

Rules change. These figures are for flights disrupted in 2026.
