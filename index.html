Questions

How long do I have to claim?

It depends on the country you flew from. UK and Ireland: 6 years. France, Spain and Greece: 5 years. Germany, Austria, Portugal and Denmark: 3 years. Italy, Malta and the Netherlands: 2 years. Some countries give you only two years, so do not sit on it.

The airline blamed the weather. Now what?

Ask them to prove it. If other aircraft were taking off and landing normally at that airport that day, the weather excuse gets weak. They have to show the disruption could not have been avoided.

I was 12 hours late, not 3. Do I get more?

No. The amount depends only on how far you flew. Three hours late and twelve hours late pay the same. What matters is that you arrived at least three hours behind schedule.

My connecting flight was the problem. Which delay counts?

The one at the end. Count how late you arrived at your final destination, not at the airport where you changed planes.

Can I claim for my child or baby?

A child with their own seat gets the same amount as an adult. A baby on your lap counts too if you paid anything for them, even a small admin fee. A UK court awarded compensation to an infant on that basis in 2017. A baby who flew completely free is not covered.

Amounts follow EU Regulation 261/2004. Distances are great-circle estimates.
